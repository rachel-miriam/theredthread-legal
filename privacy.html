Privacy Policy

Updated June 2026

This policy explains what The Red Thread (“we”, “the app”), operated by Rachel Forbes, collects, why, and your choices. We keep it minimal: we only collect what the app needs to work.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address (and, if you use social sign-in, the basic identifier that provider returns).
  • Content you create: ratings, watchlist entries, content-warning votes, tags, and reports.
  • Limited technical data needed to run the service (e.g. authentication tokens and basic error logs).

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

To provide your account, sync your ratings and watchlist across devices, power community features, keep the catalog and content warnings accurate, and fix bugs. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Beta waitlist

During our invite-only beta, you can join a waitlist by giving us your email address. We use it only to send you a confirmation, to let you know when a spot opens up, and to deliver your access code — not for marketing, and we do not share it. You can ask us to remove you at any time at support.theredthread@gmail.com.

Service providers

Your account and content are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase). Catalog and availability data come from third-party sources (Movie of the Night, TVmaze, Wikidata) — those services receive catalog identifiers, never your personal data.

Your choices

You can edit or remove your ratings, watchlist, tags, and votes at any time in the app. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your device, passwords are hashed (we never see them), and access to your data is restricted to your own account. If a data breach ever affects your information, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by law.

Children

The Red Thread is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email support.theredthread@gmail.com (or use the Send Feedback option in the app), and we’ll follow up.
