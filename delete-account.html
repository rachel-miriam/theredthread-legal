Delete Your Account

Updated June 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data in The Red Thread, the true-crime & cult documentary app operated by Rachel Forbes.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open The Red Thread and make sure you’re signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and irreversibly — there is nothing else you need to do.

Can’t access the app?

Email support.theredthread@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account.” We’ll verify it’s you and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all data associated with it:

  • Your account and login (email address and any social-sign-in identifier).
  • Your username and profile.
  • Your ratings, watchlist, and reviews.
  • Your content-warning votes, tags, and reports you submitted.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Anonymized, aggregated data that no longer identifies you may be retained — for example, a documentary’s overall average rating, which is recalculated without your individual entry.
  • Encrypted backups may briefly contain residual copies of your data; these are purged within 30 days of deletion.
  • We may retain limited records where required to comply with legal obligations.

Contact

Questions about deletion or your data? Email support.theredthread@gmail.com and we’ll follow up.
